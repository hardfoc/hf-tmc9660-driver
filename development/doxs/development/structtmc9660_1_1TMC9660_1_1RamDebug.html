◆ configure()

template<typename CommType >
bool TMC9660::RamDebug::configure ( const CaptureConfig & config)
noexcept

Reset and configure a capture without arming it.

Sends, in the vendor order (PyTrinamic RAMDebug.start_measurement): reset (0), sample count (1), prescaler (3), channels (4), pretrigger count (13), and for a conditional trigger mask/shift (6) and trigger channel (5). Arm with start().

Returns
false on an invalid config (no channels, too many channels, zero samples, pretrigger > samples, shift > 15) or a TMCL error.

noexcept

Type 6: trigger mask (VALUE) and shift (MOTOR/BANK).

The shift travels in the 4-bit MOTOR/BANK field of the frame, so only 0…15 can be encoded; larger shifts are rejected (verify against datasheet whether the firmware reads more bits).
