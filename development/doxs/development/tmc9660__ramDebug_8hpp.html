Detailed Description

RAMDebug register definitions and utilities for TMC9660.

The RAMDebug block uses a composite register offset where the upper 4 bits encode the subcommand and the lower 6 bits encode the index. All offsets are within block number 31 for SPI (command 142 for UART).
