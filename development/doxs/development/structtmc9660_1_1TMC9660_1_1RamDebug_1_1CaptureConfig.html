Detailed Description

template<typename CommType>
struct tmc9660::TMC9660< CommType >::RamDebug::CaptureConfig

Full capture configuration for configure().

Sample counts are per channel; the driver multiplies by the channel count because the chip expects totals ("not per-channel", types 1 and 13).
